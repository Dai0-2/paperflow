01 / BROWSER READER

Read PDFs where you found them

PaperFlow opens arXiv inside the original URL and handles direct or local PDFs in its integrated Reader.

  • Page thumbnails, outline, search, zoom, download, and print
  • Fast first-page rendering for remote papers
  • Reading position remembered per paper
02 / ANNOTATION

Keep evidence attached to the page

Mark the source directly instead of moving fragments into a disconnected note.

  • Highlight, underline, strikeout, text, area, and ink tools
  • Colors, comments, and zoom-safe positioning
  • Export annotated PDF, JSON, or Markdown
03 / LIBRARY

Classify without losing momentum

A dense, searchable library keeps papers, metadata, notes, and reading history together.

  • Nested collections with direct drag and drop
  • Tags, favorites, duplicates, and metadata editing
  • Fast filtering and recently read views
04 / PAPERFLOW AI

Ask with the paper in context

The AI workspace stays beside the source and keeps each conversation attached to its paper.

  • Summarize, translate, explain, and inspect methodology
  • Page-aware citations return to the evidence
  • Streaming answers, multiple threads, and paper memory
05 / GOOGLE DRIVE SYNC

Move between computers with one account

Sign in once. PaperFlow encrypts your research data locally and stores it in your own Google Drive.

  • Library, notes, annotations, conversations, and reading progress
  • Only the minimum drive.file permission
  • PDF backup remains optional and off by default
